cx works out what to do and does it with your plugins. A proposal comes with one line of reason and its source, and the whole analysis when you ask. A file is named by its title, never pasted into the conversation.

Some things you can ask:

- Why do you suggest lowering the price?
- Show me the whole analysis.
- What can you do for me right now?

cx works on cx's computer, which is used by your cx alone, and offers only what your plugins let it do.

## Plugins

Each plugin gives cx one more capability, such as a data source, a model or a skill. Once you install it on the Plugins page, cx can use it, and the plugins it needs are installed with it.
